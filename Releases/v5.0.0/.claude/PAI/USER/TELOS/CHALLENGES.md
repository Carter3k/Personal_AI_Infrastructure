# Challenges

> Personal patterns, friction points, and persistent failure modes you're aware of.

- **C0 (Neurotype — load-bearing):** AuDHD (Autism + ADHD) — diagnosed after ~10 years of treatment framed as ADHD + MDD. The new diagnosis requires rebuilding systems, routines, and strategies from scratch to actually match how my brain works. Old approaches optimized for the wrong neurotype. This context is relevant to every suggestion about habits, focus, communication, and work structure.
- **C1 (Habit formation):** Standard habit advice (streaks, "just do it", fixed schedules) doesn't work with AuDHD — need approaches that account for variable executive function, sensory regulation, and interest-driven motivation.

## Notes

Challenges are different from problems-you're-solving (see PROBLEMS.md). Challenges are *about you* — procrastination patterns, energy management, emotional triggers, ways you sabotage yourself.

Naming them here gives the DA context to coach against them instead of accidentally feeding them.
