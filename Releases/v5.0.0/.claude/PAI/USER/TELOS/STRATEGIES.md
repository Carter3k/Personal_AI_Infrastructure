# Strategies

> How you approach your missions, goals, and problems — the playbook.

- **S0 (Stay in pulse):** Maintain active contact with communities and groups — social listening, trend investigation, public sentiment measurement — to spot unserved needs before they're obvious. Proximity to people, not just data.
- **S1 (Human creativity at every step):** Design thinking, divergent thinking, crowdsourcing, and open ideation at every stage — HUMAN CREATIVITY AT EVERY STEP — before converging or automating. Never skip the messy generative phase.
- **S2 (Exploit the moment):** We're in a window of civilization-altering technological change with an abundance of unserved markets. Frugal tech, ubiquitous materials, rapidly advancing AI/robotics/fabrication capabilities — small teams can now access what institutions owned before. Position at the leading edge of what's newly possible, not what's already established.
- **S3 (Data as sensing):** Web scraping, aggregation, and sentiment analysis as practical tools — not just interesting tech — for staying ahead of cultural and market shifts and informing design decisions with real signal.

## Notes

Strategies are the *how* that connects goals to missions. A strategy is durable across specific goals — if you changed all your goals, your strategies might still apply.

---
*The DA uses strategies to suggest *methods* aligned with how you actually operate, not generic best practices that don't match your style.*
